welcome to sunset

Where San Antonio Gathers

Sunset Food Park is more than a place to eat — it's where families, friends, and food lovers come together.

From hand-crafted tacos to wood-fired pizza, we host a rotating lineup of San Antonio's best independent food trucks. Pair your meal with a cold drink from our bar, let the kids run loose at the playground, and bring your pup along for the ride. We're open daily and ready for you.

Become a Vendor

Bring your food truck to Sunset and tap into San Antonio's hungriest crowd. We have flexible options for new and established vendors.

OPTION 1

Rent A Spot

Already have a food truck? Bring it to Sunset and start serving. Get access to our built-in crowd, full bar, family-friendly amenities, and prime Austin Hwy location.

  • Established customer base
  • Prime Austin Hwy visibility
  • Shared bar & bathroom facilities
  • Family-friendly environment

Rent or Buy a Trailer

Dream of starting your own food business but don't have a truck? We offer fully-equipped trailers for rent or purchase — get cooking without the upfront investment.
